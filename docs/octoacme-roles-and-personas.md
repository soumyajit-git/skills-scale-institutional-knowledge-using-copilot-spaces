# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## UX/Design Lead

### Role Summary
UX/Design Leads own the user experience, design systems, and usability validation. They collaborate with Product Managers and Developers to ensure solutions are intuitive, accessible, and aligned with brand standards.

### Responsibilities
- Design solutions that meet user needs and acceptance criteria
- Maintain and evolve design systems and component libraries
- Conduct usability testing and incorporate user feedback
- Participate in design reviews and provide feedback to developers
- Document design decisions and rationale

### Goals
- Deliver intuitive, accessible user experiences
- Reduce support burden through better UX
- Maintain consistency across products

### Typical Communication
- Design review sessions with developers and product managers
- Usability testing feedback and research findings
- Design system documentation and component specifications

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Provides design specifications and participates in code reviews to ensure design fidelity; receives technical feasibility feedback
- **With Product Managers**: Validates design solutions against user needs and success metrics; collaborates on acceptance criteria
- **With Project Managers**: Communicates design timelines and dependencies; flags UX-related risks or blockers
- **With QA Engineers**: Works with QA to define acceptance criteria for usability and design compliance; participates in user acceptance testing

---

## QA Engineer / Tester

### Role Summary
QA Engineers define and execute quality assurance strategies, including test planning, manual testing, and acceptance validation. They ensure features meet acceptance criteria and quality standards before release.

### Responsibilities
- Develop test plans and test cases based on acceptance criteria
- Execute manual and automated testing
- Validate features against Definition of Done
- Identify and document defects with reproduction steps
- Participate in release readiness reviews

### Goals
- Ensure high-quality releases
- Reduce post-release defects
- Build confidence in feature readiness

### Typical Communication
- Test plan documentation and test case specifications
- Defect reports and regression testing updates
- Release readiness checklists and quality metrics

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Reviews acceptance criteria; reports defects with clear reproduction steps; collaborates on edge cases and test coverage
- **With Product Managers**: Clarifies acceptance criteria; validates that features meet success metrics; provides quality signals for go/no-go decisions
- **With Project Managers**: Reports test progress and quality risks; flags blockers that impact release timelines
- **With UX/Design Lead**: Validates design implementation against specifications; participates in user acceptance testing

---

## Technical Architect

### Role Summary
Technical Architects design solutions that are scalable, maintainable, and aligned with system constraints. They work with Developers and Product Managers to assess feasibility and guide technical decisions.

### Responsibilities
- Review and provide feedback on solution design
- Assess technical feasibility and risks
- Guide architectural decisions and trade-offs
- Identify integration points and dependencies
- Support teams in resolving technical blockers

### Goals
- Build scalable, maintainable systems
- Reduce technical debt and rework
- Enable faster delivery through good design

### Typical Communication
- Technical design reviews and architecture documentation
- Risk assessments and feasibility evaluations
- Technical decision records and trade-off analyses

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Developers**: Reviews technical design; advises on implementation approaches; helps resolve architectural questions and technical blockers
- **With Product Managers**: Assesses feasibility of proposed features; identifies technical trade-offs that affect scope or timeline
- **With Project Managers**: Escalates technical risks and dependencies; provides estimates for architectural work
- **With Project Managers**: Communicates architectural timelines and integration dependencies that impact the project schedule

---

## Stakeholder Liaison

### Role Summary
Stakeholder Liaisons serve as communication bridges between product delivery teams and external stakeholders (e.g., executive sponsors, customers, support). They ensure alignment, gather feedback, and communicate progress.

### Responsibilities
- Identify stakeholder needs and communicate them to the product team
- Gather and relay stakeholder feedback on proposed solutions
- Provide regular progress updates and manage expectations
- Escalate risks and blockers that impact stakeholders
- Coordinate feedback and review cycles with stakeholders

### Goals
- Maintain stakeholder alignment and engagement
- Reduce surprises and scope changes late in delivery
- Accelerate decision-making through clear communication

### Typical Communication
- Stakeholder updates and progress reports
- Feedback collection and synthesis for the product team
- Escalation communications and risk notifications

### Interaction with Existing Roles
- **With Project Managers**: Provides stakeholder input on priorities and risks; escalates stakeholder-impacting blockers; collaborates on risk communication
- **With Product Managers**: Shares customer feedback and market signals; validates product direction with key stakeholders; coordinates feature review sessions
- **With Developers & Technical Teams**: Translates technical updates into stakeholder-friendly language; manages expectations on timelines and technical constraints
- **With QA Engineers**: Coordinates user acceptance testing with stakeholders; gathers feedback from end-user testing sessions
